EVALUATION NOTES

- Pixel Accuracy measures the proportion of correctly classified pixels.
- Mean IoU measures the average intersection-over-union across classes.
- Mean Dice measures overlap between predicted and ground-truth masks.
- The SegFormer test mIoU is the reported held-out test result.
- U-Net and DeepLabV3+ aggregate metrics should be added when available.
